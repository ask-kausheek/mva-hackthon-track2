# AI Assistance and Data Handling

GitHub Copilot CLI for this package-building sessions assisted with code, literature retrieval, evidence structuring, drafting and
tests. The plan has no training on customer content.

External literature queries during package preparation concerned public gene/drug mechanisms and regulatory sources, not patient identifiers or
genotype-scale data.

The delivered pipeline contains no LLM integration or network requests. No raw VCF, FASTQ, case-wide genotype tables, credentials or stored
conversation logs are included. Its evidence consists of paraphrased public-source claims and citations and no score establishes a medicine's
effectiveness. Reconfirm applicable no-training and time/purpose-limited retention terms before further hosted processing of controlled case data.
