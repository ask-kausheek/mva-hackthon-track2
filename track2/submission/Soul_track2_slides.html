The research question

A working hypothesis, not a prescription

  • Prior Track 1 evidence identifies a putative BUB1B variant pair.
  • Can an approved medicine improve residual protein function?
  • Normal-cell benefit must not be confused with tumor-cell support.

Sources: [R1]

Allele-aware mechanism

More protein is not necessarily better function

  • Some other BUBR1 missense mutants are unstable but retain function.
  • Genetic overexpression restored checkpoint activity in selected models.
  • Case-specific transcript fate, residual function and phase remain unresolved.

Sources: [R1] | [R2]

Reproducible computation

Evidence must support each declared step

  • Seven public sources; 20 manually curated claims.
  • Required mechanism steps + drug-tested evidence -> weakest-link index.
  • Approval, conflicts and safety holds constrain ranking; ties stay tied.

Actual pipeline results

Two conditional hypotheses; no drug winner

  • Arimoclomol: indirect chaperone-support hypothesis.
  • Prescription niacin: indirect NAD / SIRT2 hypothesis.
  • Neither has qualifying drug-mediated BUBR1 functional-rescue evidence.

Sensitivity and falsification

Study removal exposes fragility

  • Three weight profiles crossed with four study removals plus the intact corpus.
  • Missing indispensable pathway evidence defers the affected hypothesis.
  • Proposed follow-up: functional segregation, viability and tumor-context controls.

Contribution and boundaries

An auditable route to the next experiment

  • Reusable code, claim-level citations, held alternatives and a machine-checked prompt pack.
  • No clinical efficacy, completed experiments or comprehensive drug screen claimed.
  • Next: verify the allele mechanism and test the most defensible evidence chain.
